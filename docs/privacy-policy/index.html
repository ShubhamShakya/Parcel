Parcel

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.shubhamshakya.parcel

The short version

  • Parcel keeps everything you enter on your own device, in a local database.
  • There is no Parcel account server. Nothing you enter is uploaded to us, because there is nowhere to upload it to.
  • We do not collect analytics, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • Parcel is a shopping simulator. No real money is charged and nothing is shipped, so no real payment or delivery details are processed.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything.

Who this policy is from

Parcel is published by Shubham Shakya. This policy explains how the Parcel Android app handles information. It covers the app only — not any other app or website.

What Parcel stores on your device

All of the following is written to a database inside the app's own private storage area on your phone. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhy it exists
Phone number and the first and last name you provide at sign-in To create a local profile so the app can greet you and keep your shopping separate between profiles on the same device. No SMS is sent and the code is not verified against any server.
An email address, if you choose to add one in Edit profile Shown back to you in your profile. Optional, and no mail is ever sent to it.
Delivery addresses you add or edit To fill in the simulated checkout. The app asks you to use fictional details, and ships nothing to them.
Your shopping activity in the app Wishlist, cart contents, simulated orders and receipts, delivery progress, unboxed items and shelf placements, simulated returns, delivery ratings, in-app support chat messages, and which notifications you have read.
App settings Your appearance choice (System, Light or Dark) and the fact that you accepted the simulation disclosure.

Earlier versions of the app offered an optional date-of-birth field. It is no longer shown or requested. If you entered one previously, the stored value stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What Parcel does not collect

  • No location. The app requests no location permission and has no access to GPS. The delivery map and "delivery location" screens are illustrations driven by simulated progress, not by where you are.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or calendar. The app requests none of these permissions.
  • No real payment details. See Payments below.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising identifiers. No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is active in the app.
  • No device identifiers sent to us. We operate no server that receives data from the app.

Permissions

Parcel declares a single Android permission: INTERNET. It is required because the app bundles payment and advertising libraries that need network access when they are switched on. In the current release neither is switched on, and ordinary use of the app — browsing, wishlist, cart, simulated checkout, order tracking, unboxing and the shelf — works entirely offline.

Payments

Parcel does not take payments. Orders are completed with a local demo confirmation that moves no money and charges nothing. The "payment methods" shown in the app are fixed demonstration labels built into the app, not real cards, and the app never stores card numbers, UPI identifiers, bank details or any other financial information.

The app does include PayU's CheckoutPro library so that a test-mode sandbox checkout can be developed. It is disabled in this release and the option is not shown at checkout. If a future release enables it, payment details would be entered inside PayU's own screens and handled under PayU's privacy policy, never stored by Parcel — and this policy will be updated before that release ships.

Advertising

This release shows no advertisements. Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform libraries are bundled in the app but are never started, so they request no advertising identifier and load no ads.

If a future release turns advertising on, Google's advertising services would collect data such as an advertising identifier and device information under Google's policies, a consent prompt would be shown where required, and this policy and the Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that release ships.

Backups and transfers

Automatic Android cloud backup is turned off for Parcel, and every storage area is excluded from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Your Parcel data therefore stays on the device it was created on and is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You are always in control:

  • Log out from Profile to end the session. Your profile stays on the device so you can log back in.
  • Delete individual items at any time — remove addresses, clear your cart or remove wishlist items from within the app.
  • Clear all app data in Android Settings → Apps → Parcel → Storage → Clear storage. This erases the entire local database, including profiles, addresses and order history.
  • Uninstall Parcel to remove all of it at once.

Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on request and no data export for us to provide — clearing the app's storage is complete and final.

Shared data between profiles on one device

If more than one phone number is used to sign in on the same installation, profiles and names are kept separately, but shopping data — wishlist, cart, addresses, orders and the shelf — is shared across profiles on that installation. Anyone who can unlock the device can therefore see that shopping data. Do not enter information you would not want another user of the same device to see.

Children

Parcel is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app stores everything locally and transmits nothing, a parent or guardian can remove all information a child has entered by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes — in particular enabling advertising or real payments — will be reflected here, and in the Play Data safety disclosure, before the release that makes them.
